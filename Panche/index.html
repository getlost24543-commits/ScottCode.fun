Four billion years ago nothing here was looking up. Now we send machines past the edge of the Sun, and every one of them started as someone staring at a dark sky and asking what is out there.

Five places worth the trip

Moon

1.3 light-seconds away. Footprints from 1969 are still there.

Mars

Rust-red dust, ancient riverbeds and the tallest volcano known.

Europa

A shell of ice over an ocean with more water than all of Earth's.

Titan

Methane rain, orange haze and lakes you could not drink.

Saturn

Rings 280,000 km wide and sometimes only ten metres thick.

Liftoff

The first nine minutes use most of the fuel.

Max Q

Peak stress on the rocket. Then the air thins and it gets easy.

Coast

No engines needed. Momentum carries you for months.

Burn

One precise push to drop into orbit around a new world.
