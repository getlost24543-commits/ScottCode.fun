# May Panche — interactive glass garden

A standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript page. No build step or framework is required.

## Controls
- Tap one of the four small dots around the circular dial to select Frost, Glass colour, Sun temperature, or Scene.
- Drag around the outside of the dial to change the selected setting. Tap the centre to cycle through settings.
- Drag across the frosted glass to wipe a clear trail. Tap the glass once to clear all frost.
- Use Wind, Sound, and Reset in the footer.

## Run
Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or upload the complete `may-panche` folder to a static host. Keep `assets/garden.svg`, `styles.css`, and `app.js` alongside `index.html` in their existing paths.
