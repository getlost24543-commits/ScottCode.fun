Mission log

  1. 1957

    Sputnik 1 becomes the first artificial satellite. The race begins.

  2. 1969

    Apollo 11 lands two people on the Moon. Six hundred million watched.

  3. 1977

    Voyager 1 and 2 launch. Voyager 1 is now the farthest human-made object.

  4. 1990

    Hubble opens its eye. Deep field images reveal thousands of galaxies in a dark patch.

  5. 2012

    Curiosity touches down on Mars after a sky-crane descent.

  6. 2021

    The James Webb Space Telescope launches and unfolds a mirror the size of a tennis court.
